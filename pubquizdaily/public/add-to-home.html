Add Pub Quiz Daily to your home screen

It takes about 10 seconds and means you'll never miss a question. No app store needed.

1
Open pubquizdaily.com in your browser
Visit pubquizdaily.com in your phone's browser.
2
Tap the Share button
Look for the ⎙ icon at the bottom of the screen — a box with an arrow pointing up. Tap it.
3
Choose "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the menu and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add in the top right. Done!
1
Open Pub Quiz Daily in Chrome
Make sure you're using Chrome — it works best for adding to your home screen on Android.
2
Tap the three-dot menu
Look for the ⋮ icon in the top right corner of Chrome. Tap it to open the menu.
3
Choose "Add to Home screen"
Tap Add to Home screen from the menu, then tap Add. Pub Quiz Daily will appear on your home screen like an app.
🍺
That's it!
Pub Quiz Daily will appear on your home screen. Tap it every day for your daily questions.
